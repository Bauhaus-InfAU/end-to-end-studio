Session 3Skills

Twenty skills, one entry

Each of you writes one skill. The lead, competition-proposal, runs them in rounds: every skill whose inputs exist starts at the same time, each as its own agent, and the next round starts when they are done. Your Reads: line decides your round. Click a skill to see what it needs and who waits for it.

Every skill also reads brief/, proposal/site.geojson and the site data from site-kit. The ribbons show only what the Reads: lines add.

Reads

The brief, the site, the site data, and the skills on your Reads: line. Nothing you didn't list is guaranteed to exist when you start.

Writes

Everything into proposal/<your-skill>/ and nowhere else. Drawings as SVG with a PNG next to each, images as PNG, tables as CSV.

Leaves

A summary.md of three lines: what you found or made, the one number that matters, and the one thing you're unsure of.
