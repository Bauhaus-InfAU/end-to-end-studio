# Phasing

Reads: structure-plan, massing, programme

The structure plan gives its neighbourhoods and centres a phase and the homes it plans; the massing builds them, and its buildings may carry a phase of their own. This skill makes them one answer: every new building gets one phase, each phase counts the homes it really holds, and its numbers are the homes and residents per phase that panels and texts show. Take the brief's rules on phases (which part comes first, a minimum of homes per phase, what must be built by when) from its checklist, and the brief only at the pages it names; never write a place or a number into the skill.

Work in the proposal folder you are told, else the newest in the project's `proposals/`, and write only into `phasing/`. Copy `${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/scripts/config.example.json` to `phasing/config.json` and fill it, each value with its source: the floor area per home the massing used, so both count homes alike, unless the brief gives one; the residents per home from the programme; the brief's minimum homes per phase and what must come first. Where neither the structure plan nor the massing phases a new neighbourhood, decide it in `phase_of` from the brief's order, with the reason. Then run `python "${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/scripts/phases.py" --proposal <proposal folder> --config <proposal>/phasing/config.json` (`pip install shapely pyproj`, no Rhino). A building takes its phase from `phase_of`, else from its structure-plan neighbourhood or centre, else from the massing; its homes are its housing floor area over the floor area per home, without a mixed building's ground floor or a daycare's floors.

It writes `phases.csv` (per phase: homes, residents, the structure plan's planned homes and the difference, the minimum, its neighbourhoods, and what comes first: its centres and community buildings), `phases.geojson` (a `phase` area per phase, and every new building with its `phase` and `homes`) and `stats.json`, whose `flags` name every mismatch: a phase whose homes differ from the plan's, buildings the massing phases differently, buildings with no phase, a phase below its minimum, something due first that isn't there. Never change another skill's file; if the design should change, say what. Write `summary.md` in three lines: the phases and what comes first in each; the homes and residents per phase; every flag, the neighbourhood furthest from its planned homes, and what you're unsure of.
