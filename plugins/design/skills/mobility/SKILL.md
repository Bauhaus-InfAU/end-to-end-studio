# Mobility

Reads: brief-reader, structure-plan, massing, streets, drawing-graphics, panel-layout

Take the site and its streets from `site.geojson` and its layers, the public transport, allowed accesses, forbidden links and scales from the brief (the plan's scale and what it must show from its frame in `panel-layout/layout.json` when there is one), and the neighbourhoods and streets from the other skills; cite the brief's page for each rule. Work in Python with `shapely`, never writing a place or number into the skill.

Start from the public transport the site and the brief give (a station, stops, lines) and make the main stop, if there is one, the point the routes meet; place hubs only where the brief or the programme asks for them; with neither a main stop nor a hub, plan the routes without them. Keep cars to the edge where the brief allows, on short access streets with no through-route. Draw a few direct main bike routes, each leading to a named place beyond the site, plus local ones, and for pedestrians the main links to the stops and centres and the paths along the edges and through the neighbourhoods. Take the street surface and the centrelines from `streets/streets.geojson` when it exists, for the plan's base and the walking network.

Work in the proposal folder you are told, else the newest in the project's `proposals/`, and write only into `mobility/`. Copy `${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/scripts/config.example.json` to `mobility/config.json` and fill it from the brief, with the page of each: the hub and the main stop (left out when the brief has none), the walking limits from the brief, else a cited norm, the gateways where cars may enter, the links cars must not make, the bike routes and the named places they lead to, and the modes of a route whose words are unclear. Then run `python "${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/scripts/mobility.py" --proposal <proposal folder> --site <site folder> --config <proposal>/mobility/config.json --style <proposal>/drawing-graphics/style.json` (`pip install shapely pyproj networkx`; Chrome or Edge for the PNG). It writes `mobility.geojson` (`route` by mode and level, `stop`, `hub`, `access`), the plan as SVG and PNG, `walk.json` and `stats.json`. Read `stats.json`, look at the PNG once, then write `mobility.md` with each move and its source, and a three-line `summary.md` whose number is the share of new housing within walking distance of the main stop, else of the nearest stop, or none without stops.
