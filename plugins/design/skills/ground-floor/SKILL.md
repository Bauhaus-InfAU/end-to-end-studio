# Ground floor

Reads: brief-reader, massing, programme, structure-plan, mobility, open-space, streets

The massing gives every new building its `ground_floor` use and owns it; this skill adds what a detail plan needs on the ground, by rules, never placed by hand. Take the brief's numbers (bus bays or platforms at a hub, bike spaces, play areas) from its checklist, and the brief only at the pages it names; never write a place or a number into the skill. Never change another skill's file: a use refined here (a food market put into a shop floor at a centre) is written into this skill's file only, and a change the massing should make goes in `summary.md`.

Work in the proposal folder you are told, else the newest in the project's `proposals/`, and write only into `ground-floor/`. Copy `${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/scripts/config.example.json` to `ground-floor/config.json` and fill it, each value with its page or the reason for an assumption: `centre_uses`, the uses with an area that the programme and the structure plan's `holds` put into a centre's ground floors; `hubs`, the bus bays and bike spaces the brief asks for at a hub of `mobility/` or a centre; the bike stands per door and a courtyard's play area. Then run `python "${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/scripts/ground_floor.py" --proposal <proposal folder> --config <proposal>/ground-floor/config.json` (`pip install shapely pyproj`, no Rhino). Each building gets its doors on the facade nearest a street (a path where no street is near), one per 30 m of front, with bike stands beside each; each courtyard large enough and without a playground gets a play area; each building at a centre is labelled with its ground floor's use and area, a `centre_uses` entry taking the shop floor that fits it; the bus bays go along the street nearest the hub and the bike parking nearest its stop, on the hub's squares and centre.

It writes `detail.geojson` (`entrance` points with `bearing_deg` facing out, `bike-stands` lines, `play` areas, `use` points with a `label`, `bus-bay` and `bike-parking` areas), `uses.csv` and `stats.json`, whose `flags` name every use or surface that found no room; the plans skill draws it in the detail areas. Write `summary.md` in three lines: what was placed (doors, stands, play areas, labelled uses, bays and bike spaces); the ground-floor area of the non-housing uses at the centres; every flag, and that the stands and play areas are illustrative proposals, not the brief's numbers.
