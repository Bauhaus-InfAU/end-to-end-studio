# Concept

Skills that decide what the entry is about, before anything is drawn.

- Input: the brief's checklist, the site and the first analysis.
- Output: text as Markdown, tables as CSV, the urban structure as GeoJSON. No drawings: pictograms of the idea belong to `drawings`.
- A new skill here: another decision that comes before the design, such as a stakeholder map or a set of design principles.
