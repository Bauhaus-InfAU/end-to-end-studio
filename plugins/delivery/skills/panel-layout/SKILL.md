# Panel layout

Reads: everything

Work in the proposal folder you are given, else the newest in the project's `proposals/`, else a new `proposals/<YYYY-MM-DD>_draft/`, never the project's top folder; write only to its `panel-layout/`. Needs Python 3.11+ and Chrome, Edge or Chromium (no other installs). First run `python "${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/scripts/panels.py" scan <proposal folder>`: it lists every summary, drawing (with its print size and the scale its skill gives), table and text, and the inputs that are missing. Read every `summary.md`, the storyline and title from `narrative/`, and the format from `brief-reader/checklist.md`. If there is no checklist, read the format from the brief itself (where the project's `CLAUDE.md` says, else `brief/`): panel size and count, orientation, file limit, reserved boxes such as a code field, panel numbers, names and titles that must appear, the drawings and figures it asks for, and its language. Read the whole brief for these, not only its general submission rules: a site's own list of drawings, scales and figures often comes later, in the task or an appendix. Cite the PDF page for each, not the printed page number. The look comes from `drawing-graphics/standard.md`: fonts, colours, line weights. Without it, leave `style` out: the script's default is black on white with one accent colour. Never pick a look of your own.

Then write `panel-layout/layout.json` as `${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/layout.md` describes, and run `python "${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/scripts/panels.py" render <proposal folder>/panel-layout/layout.json`. One panel per step of the storyline; without a storyline, the order the brief lists its drawings in, from the large scale to the small. A drawing with a scale is printed at its true size, never shrunk or stretched to fit: if it doesn't fit, move it or give it a panel of its own; if it still doesn't fit, keep it in `layout.json` anyway, so the report names it as not fitting rather than missing. Leave reserved boxes empty. Each panel says a few things large rather than everything small. Every number on a panel shows its source; take the numbers from the tables and summaries, never make one up. If two numbers that should agree don't (such as a total and the sum of its phases), show both with their sources, name the mismatch in `notes` and in `summary.md`, and never correct either. Text belongs to `narrative`: use its words, shortened if needed, not your own.

Read `panel-layout/report.md` and look at every `panel-N.png`. Fix what doesn't fit, what overflows and what overlaps; where a panel is too empty because a drawing is missing, leave the space and say so; never blow up a diagram or add content of your own to fill it. Render again, at most three more times. What the brief asks for and nobody made stays listed as missing in the report: don't draw it yourself. End with `summary.md` in three lines: the panels made and what they show; the one number that matters (how many of the brief's required items are on the panels); what you're unsure of.
