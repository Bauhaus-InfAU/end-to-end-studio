# Delivery

Skills that turn the parts into an entry and read it as others will.

- Input: everything in the proposal folder. `panel-layout` reads every summary and every drawing, so a skill added anywhere shows up without a change here.
- Output: the panels in the format the brief asks for, as PDF and PNG; the jury's report as Markdown.
- A new skill here: another way of handing in or reviewing, such as a short film, a booklet, or a second jury with another focus.
