Mobility plan for Regensburg Nord: the new Wutzlhofen stop with a 6-platform bus station (line A from Sandgasse, line B from Chamer Strasse, a bus gate so no street links the two), cars kept to four edge entries and 1,050 district-garage spaces, 5 main bike routes each to a named place (city centre, Haslbach, west landscape via the B16 underpass, Schwarzholz, Brandlberg), foot links to every centre; files mobility.geojson, mobility.md, mobility_1-3000 and the hub detail plan mobility_hub_1-1000 (svg/png), scripts/.
Walking from the stop along our routes: 8 of 93 new housing buildings are within 400 m and 56 within 800 m (median 695 m), centres 81 to 1,143 m away; the 400/800 m norm (VDV) is general practice, not the brief's.
Unsure: stop taken at the Station Square (the structure plan's gateway lies 250 m south), garage spaces (about 0.4 per home), bus C, the bus gate and filter positions are assumptions; no drawing-graphics standard, so the fallback look.
