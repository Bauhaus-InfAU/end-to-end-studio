Half Field as a structure plan (`structure-plan.geojson`, `checks.txt`, `check-plan.png`; made by `design.py`, numbers from `measure.py`): six new islands, each under 400 m across (largest 397 m), take 31.9 ha with the kept Konradsiedlung edge, the Wutzlhofen hamlet and the Chamer and Haslbach works. They sit on one field of 58.0 ha (51.4 % of the site once the 19.4 ha of street land and rail are left out). The field holds the cold-air corridor, the canal and 150 kV strips, the basin, the biotopes, the solar park, the woods and the B16 strip. Main centre: Station square (stop, 6-platform bus station, district garage, market); four local centres, including the school and the Nordbad. That makes 3,278 against the framework plan's 2,800 and below the BauNVO maximum of 3,400, and about 2,620 / 1,780 / 1,470 residents at the programme's 1.79 per home. No analysis/ existed, so the site was read from the brief, its plans pp. 36-37 (traced, residuals 7-16 m), climate/ and the site layers. site.geojson is 112.8 ha against the brief's "about 107 ha".
Walking along the site's streets and paths and the plan's routes: 71 % of new neighbourhood land is within 400 m of a centre, and 100 % is within 150 m of a centre or public green (20 m grid; straight-line check in checks.txt: 100 %). Rules not fully met: the Brandlberg lane crosses about 230 m of field edge between the sports park and the compensation strip to reach Haidhofweg, and the Chamer and Haslbach works (kept, 494 m across) are not held to the 400 m rule.
