Placed 141 new buildings on the structure plan's 10 new neighbourhoods. The homes stand as rows and open courtyard blocks with their long sides on the 8/188 deg cold-air axis (narrative principle 4), mostly 5 to 6 storeys, with 2 landmarks of 10 storeys at Station Rise; one of them holds the food market. Commerce is in 27 halls in Rail Works East and Haslbach Edge, plus 1 building in the 65 dB(A) strip. The school with its yard and sports ground is on Canal School, the Nordbad pool on Station East, and the 3 daycares (1,000 m2 each, 950 m2 grounds) on the centres the structure plan names for them. Also 48 greened secondary streets, 1,363 street trees, 8 squares and 67 courtyard gardens; the 209 buildings already on the site are kept. Phase 1 holds about 1,315 homes against the at least 1,100 asked (p. 33); the phases come from structure-plan.geojson. Files: massing.geojson, areas.csv, stats.json, assumptions.md, massing.svg/.png (check plan 1:3,000, 621 x 672 mm, the entry's standard with the field line) and massing_3d.svg/.png.

Commerce: 180,552 m2 against 230,000 m2 (p. 30), 49,448 m2 short, and all 28 commerce buildings already stand at the 6-floor cap, so the structure plan would need about 2.5 ha more commercial land, or the gap stays. School 3,000 m2, yard 2,066 m2, sports 3,000 m2, pool 3,000 m2 and the daycares 3 x 1,000 m2 all meet the brief. Public green is 32.9 ha against 7.3 ha; the farmed B16 Field and Valley Field and the solar park are not counted.
