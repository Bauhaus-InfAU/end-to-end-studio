Unsure: the 2 % stop reach (the jury's straight-line measure gives 42 %, so the entry's network count may be wrong); the 150 kV and canal strips, which the entry read by eye (30 to 50 m); 112.8 ha traced against about 107 ha in the brief. No closed-competition material was seen.
