Field Edge as a figure: one 41 ha field (B16 field, a field finger and a canal field reaching into phase 1, the biotope, the valley along the rail with the cold-air corridor and the basin, joined across the rail at the kept underpass and a new station bridge) with eight housing neighbourhoods on 24.6 ha along its edge (141 homes per ha, gross with local streets), the main centre at the Wutzlhofen stop and six local centres; phase 1 (Sandgasse Slope, Chamer Slope, Station Rise, Line Corner) holds 1,350 homes against at least 1,100 asked (p. 33), phase 2 (Valley South, Corridor Terrace, Woodside) 1,400 against my 1,100, phase 3 (B16 Terrace) 720 against my 600, so 3,470 against 2,800 asked (+24 %); that is above the 3,400 BauNVO maximum, so the massing stops at 3,400. At the programme's 1.79 residents per home that is about 2,420, 2,510 and 1,290 residents.
Within 200 m walk of a public green or a centre: 98 % of the housing area (median 37 m); within 200 m walk of the field: 93 %, and 100 % in a straight line (the narrative's rule); within 400 m walk of a centre: 74 %. All are measured from a 20 m grid along the plan's routes and today's streets and paths; the neighbourhoods' own streets aren't drawn yet, so these are lower bounds.
Least sure: the 150 kV line, the canal, the stop and the solar park are read by eye from the Infrastructure Plan (p. 36, Fig. 26) to about 30 to 50 m, and they set the edges of Line Corner, Station Rise and Corridor Terrace (the solar park alone took about 170 homes from phase 2); the city's survey of the line, the canal and the solar park would settle it. Also open: site.geojson is 112.8 ha against about 107 ha in the brief (the site skill's job); two car streets cross the field where the brief fixes the access (existing Chamer Strasse across the finger, the Glashuettenstrasse access across the corridor's southern tip); Old Pilsen-Allee is flagged as running along a cycle path; 2.0 ha left unassigned is rail land; the phases are in structure-plan.geojson, but no phasing drawing is made here.
