Existing streets come from the site folder's street-land.geojson (cadastre street, square and path areas), used as they are; new streets from massing/massing.geojson, with a locally patched copy of streets.py (streets_local.py) because the original crashed on a multi-part street line (the patch only splits such lines into parts).
Streets cover 17.2% of the site (existing 69.0 ha, new 9.9 ha, 0.04 ha of existing street land removed under new buildings); check plan streets_1-3000.png/.svg at 1:3,000, geometry in streets.geojson, stats.json, assumptions.md.
Unsure: 1 building is kept under a new street and 1 main route ends in nowhere, so the massing should move that building off the street and close or link that route; 268 centrelines use default widths, and 7 ends are left as future links.
