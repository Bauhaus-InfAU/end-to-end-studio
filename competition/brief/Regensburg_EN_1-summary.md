# Regensburg_EN_1: summary

The competition brief for Europan 18, site Regensburg (Germany), from Europan Germany with the City of Regensburg, launched 3 March 2025 (p. 3). It's an open, anonymous, one-stage ideas competition (p. 8). Part 1 is the general Europan rules (pp. 8–16), Part 2 the task (pp. 22–34), followed by the city's plans (pp. 35–37). Pages are PDF pages, as in the `<!-- page N -->` markers; the printed numbers are 2 lower.

## The task

- A mixed-use, low-traffic district that is as climate-neutral as possible, balancing density, minimal sealing and climate resilience, on the idea of the "15-minute city" (p. 8).
- Project site: about 107 ha of farmland, about 4 km north of the centre, between the Haslbach commercial area (north) and the settlements Konradsiedlung-Wutzlhofen and Brandlberg (south) (pp. 24, 27).
- Session theme: "Re-Sourcing: Rethinking resources" (p. 10). The city wants a careful use of unsealed soil, and "innovative and bold ideas are expressly desired" (p. 33).
- Orientation: the "Rahmenplan Regensburg Nord" framework plan, adopted October 2024 (p. 27; plan p. 37).

## Programme (p. 30, pp. 33–34)

| | Framework plan | Maximum |
|---|---|---|
| Residents | 5,000 | 6,100 |
| Homes | 2,800 | 3,400 |
| Residential ground area | 250,000 m² | 300,000 m² |
| Public green (12 m² per resident) | 6 ha | 7.3 ha |
| Commerce, floor area (ground area 115,000 m²) | 230,000 m² | 276,000 m² |
| Social infrastructure and sport, total area | 36,500 m² | – |

- First phase: at least 1,100 homes, between Chamer Straße and the railway (detail area 1, mostly city-owned) (p. 33).
- A mix of housing types, from cluster and cross-generational housing to one-room flats and homes for more than four people (p. 33).
- District centre with a food market of about 1,200 m² sales area; a few landmark buildings over 21 m are possible there (p. 33).
- 3 daycare centres for 95 children each, at least 10 m² outdoor play per child, one in or near the first phase (pp. 30, 33).
- Primary school for about 370 children: 3,000 m² floor area, 2,000 m² schoolyard, at least 3,000 m² outdoor sports (pp. 30, 34).
- Indoor pool (Nordbad): 3,000 m² hall, 4,000 m² with its plot (p. 30, 34).
- Commerce for small and medium firms and crafts; no large office blocks (p. 34).
- Play area 1.5 m² per resident, at least 500 m² (p. 28).

## Mobility

- A new rail stop at Wutzlhofen on the Regensburg–Hof line, from 2028, is the centre of the mobility concept: a hub with a bus station with six bays, sharing, Kiss+Ride, and Park+Ride that also works as district garage (p. 27).
- City buses arrive from the south (Sandgasse), regional buses from the north (Chamer Straße) (p. 27).
- Few to no cars; several smaller mobility points with district garages; visitor parking on private land (pp. 27, 34).
- Keep the two pedestrian and cycle underpasses (under the B16, and under the railway and Pilsenallee) (pp. 27–28).

## Hard constraints (pp. 24, 28, 30)

- B16: no access from it, buildings at least 20 m from it (p. 28).
- Pilsenallee: the only connection is at Coburger Straße (p. 28).
- Vehicle access via Chamer Straße, Sandgasse and Glashüttenstraße, but no street linking Chamer Straße to Sandgasse (p. 28).
- No high-rise over 21 m, except landmarks in the district centre (pp. 28, 33). Distance to the street at least 0.4 H and at least 3 m (p. 28).
- Main sewer, north–south from Sandgasse: 10 m kept free of buildings (p. 30).
- 150 kV power line: no buildings in a 20 m strip below it (p. 30).
- Keep the rainwater retention basin southeast of the underpasses; keep the solar park on the east edge (p. 30).
- Keep and integrate the third-party properties shown in the settlement structure plan (p. 30); keep existing biotopes and compensation areas, and existing trees before planting new ones (p. 28).
- The traffic axes carry 10,000–20,000 vehicles a day: noise. Taller mixed-use buildings along Pilsenallee and the railway should shield the housing behind (pp. 24, 28).
- A cold-air corridor runs north–south through the site; buildings and green should follow the fresh-air corridors (pp. 24, 33).

## What to submit (pp. 11–12, 34)

- 3 vertical A1 panels (594 × 841 mm, PDF, max 20 MB), a blank 60 × 40 mm box top left for the code with the city name next to it, panel number top right; every drawing with a graphic scale; English, or English and German (p. 11).
- On the panels: figure-ground 1:15,000; site plan 1:3,000 with storeys; detail area 1 around the mobility hub 1:1,000; detail area 2 at 1:1,000 or 1:500 (the district centre is recommended); at least two perspectives and one isometric; soil sealing as fully and partly sealed shares in %; residents per phase; the phasing (p. 34).
- A text on A4, 3–4 pages with few visuals, with 3–4 sentences each on: concept and E18 theme, study site, project site, urban development, green and open space, mobility, uses, step-by-step development (pp. 12, 34). The brief also says "6 pages max." once (p. 11).
- For communication: 3 JPG images (max 1 MB each) and a text of 800 characters (p. 12).
- Site name and project title on every document (p. 11).

## How it's judged (p. 14)

Concept and site; the session theme, especially sustainability and adaptability; the programme against the brief; whether it can become an urban process; innovative public space; the links between uses; architectural and technical quality. The local jury shortlists at most 25 %. Prizes: winner €12,000, runner-up €6,000, special mention unpaid (p. 3).
