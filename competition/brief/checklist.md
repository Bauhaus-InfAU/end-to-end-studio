# Checklist: Europan 18, Regensburg (Regensburg_EN_1)

Page numbers are PDF pages (the printed numbers are 2 lower).

## Numbers
- Project site about 107 ha, 4 km from the city centre, now farmland (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 27)
- Homes: 2,800 to 3,400 units (framework plan 2,800; BauNVO maximum 3,400) (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 33, p. 30)
- Residents: about 5,000 to 6,100 (framework plan 5,000; maximum 6,100) (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 33, p. 30)
- First construction phase: at least 1,100 homes (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 33)
- Housing ground area: 250,000 m² (framework plan); 300,000 m² (maximum) (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 30)
- Public green: at least 12 m² per resident = 6 ha for 5,000, 7.3 ha for 6,100 (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 30, p. 34)
- Play area: 1.5 m² per inhabitant, at least 500 m² per district (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 28)
- Commerce: ground area 115,000 m²; floor area 230,000 m² (framework plan), 276,000 m² (maximum) (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 30)
- Social infrastructure and sport: 36,500 m² total (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 30)
- 3 daycare centres, each 4 groups for 95 children (285 in all), 1,000 m² floor area each, at least 10 m² outdoor play area per child; at least one in or near phase 1 (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 30, p. 33)
- Primary school, years 1 to 4, about 370 children: 3,000 m² floor area, 2,000 m² schoolyard (min. 5 m² per child), 3,000 m² open-air sports area (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 30, pp. 33–34)
- Nordbad indoor pool: 3,000 m² gross hall floor area; 4,000 m² hall plus plot (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 30, p. 34)
- Food market: about 1,200 m² sales area in the district centre (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 33)
- Central bus station with 6 platforms at the mobility point; Kiss+Ride and Park+Ride areas nearby (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 27)
- New railway stop at Wutzlhofen, to be built from 2028 (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 27)
- Phases: development in sections; phase 1 is the area between Chamer Straße and the railway line (detail area 1) (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 33)

## Rules
- Density suitable; no extensive high-rise from 21 m height (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 28)
- A few profile-forming buildings over 21 m are conceivable in the district centre (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 33)
- Distance to the street at least 0.4 of the wall height H, never under 3 m; Bavarian building rules for third parties' plots (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 28)
- The ground and floor areas of the framework plan are minimum and/or maximum values (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 28)
- B16: no turnoffs, no access; buildings at least 20 m from the street (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 28)
- Pilsenallee: no turnoffs; the only connection is at Coburger Straße (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 28)
- Streets inside may not connect Chamer Straße and Sandgasse directly (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 28)
- Access from Chamer Straße, Sandgasse and Glashüttenstraße (Brandlberg) (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 28)
- Main collector canal: 10 m protective distance free of buildings (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 30)
- 150 kV line: no buildings in a 20 m strip; other lines may go underground (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 30)
- Rainwater retention basin south-east of the rail underpasses: keep, ideally in the open space (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 30)
- Solar park on the eastern edge: may not be rezoned (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 30)
- Third parties' properties in the Settlement Structure Plan: keep and integrate (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 30)
- Two pedestrian and cycle underpasses (under B16; under railway and Pilsenallee): keep for walkers and cyclists only (Regensburg_EN_1.md pp. 27–28)
- Tree protection regulation: keep existing trees before planting new ones (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 28)
- Existing biotopes and compensation areas: integrate in the green network (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 28)
- Fresh air corridors: respect in the orientation of buildings and green (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 33)
- Few to no cars; visitor parking on private land as far as possible (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 27, p. 34)
- Barrier-free throughout (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 33)
- Avoid use-related block formation; flexible ground floors for commerce, services, gastronomy, local supply, social uses (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 33)
- Denser, taller, mixed-use buildings along Pilsenallee and the railway to shield the housing behind from noise; housing in the south (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 28, p. 33)
- Commerce for small and medium companies and crafts; large-scale offices not wanted (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 34)
- Less soil sealing; green, social and innovative district aims (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 33)
- One-stage, anonymous, open call for ideas; site name and project title on every document (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 8, p. 11)
- Panels in English, or English plus the site language (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 11)

## Panels and format
- 3 panels, A1 portrait (594 × 841 mm), PDF, at most 20 MB (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 11)
- Blank box 60 × 40 mm in the upper left corner for the code, the city name next to it (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 11)
- Panels numbered 1 to 3 in the upper right corner; title position free (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 11)
- Panels explain the urban ideas against the site issues and the session theme; show the project as a whole and its relation to the existing context, with three-dimensional views; show the implementation method (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 11)
- Text: A4 portrait PDF with the same 60 × 40 mm box, limited visuals (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 12)
- Text length unclear: "3 to 4 pages maximum" (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 12), "4 pages (max)" and "6 pages max" (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 11)
- The text answers eight points, three to four sentences each (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 34)
- Communication: 3 separate JPG images, each at most 1 MB, and a text of 800 signs with spaces (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 12)
- Eligibility documents are uploaded separately per person (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 12)
- Video of 3 minutes (MP4, H.264, English) is for winners and runners-up only (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 12)

## What the jury looks for
- Round 1 (local jury): compliance with the rules; then conceptual content and degree of innovation against the Europan 18 topic; shortlist of at most 25 % (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 14)
- Round 2 (national jury), "could assess": the relationship between concept and site (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 14)
- relevance to the topic, in particular sustainable development and adaptability (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 14)
- relevance of the programme to the general brief for the site (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 14)
- the potential for integration into an urban process adapted to the site's issue (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 14)
- the innovative nature of the proposed public spaces (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 14)
- the consideration given to the connection between different functions (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 14)
- the architectural and technical qualities (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 14)
- The city also expressly wants innovative and bold ideas (Regensburg_EN_1.md p. 33)
