## Which gaps weigh most

My own reading, before any judge:

G10 (the design) and G1 (the site plan) are the core. Every winner spends a board, or most of one, on the site plan, and ours loses there on both content and look. G2 (the detail areas) is where a jury reads the interior, and we have nothing to show there. G5 (the perspectives) is the most visible break in the look. G9 (the panels) multiplies everything else.

The causes group into three layers:

- **Geometry and design layers that hold too little.** K5, K6 and K7: no trees, only boxes, no ground floors. No drawing skill can draw what isn't there.
- **A look that can't be learnt.** K1, K2, K3, K4 and K9: references read once, for colours, into a standard the scripts can't use beyond colours.
- **A harness with no loop.** K15, K16 and K17: no step compares a result with the reference and sends it back.

Fixing only the look layer gives prettier slabs. Fixing only the geometry gives the same red diagram with more boxes. The changes in `changes.md` work on all three.
