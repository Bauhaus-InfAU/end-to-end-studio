# Feedback on run-10, 04.11.2026

In Session 4 the room put 109 stickies on run-10's three panels and on the winners' boards, on the Miro frame "Session 4: Results Review". This folder holds them, grouped into 13 issues. Next week's measure: how close the runs get to the winners' panels.

- `stickies.json`: every sticky, with the panel, the drawing under it, the skill that drew it, the issue and whether it's a problem, praise, an idea, a question or a note. `at` is the spot as a share of the panel's width and height.
- `panel-1_marked.png` to `panel-3_marked.png`: run-10's panels with each sticky's number on its spot. Coral a problem, green praise, blue an idea or a note.
- `issues/`: one file per issue, the text of the GitHub issue.
- The winners' drawings, cut one per file, and their marked boards: `competition/references/winners/` (from Moodle, not in git).
