## Lens

At a join: narrative writes the idea, but concept-diagrams and panel-layout decide what the reader sees of it. Method.

## Done means

A stranger reads panel 1 for 30 seconds and can say the idea in one sentence; each phase on panel 3 says why it comes in that order; the idea text is at most 80 words, the rest is in diagrams with notes and arrows.
